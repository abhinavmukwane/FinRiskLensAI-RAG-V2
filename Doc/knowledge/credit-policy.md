# §1 Scope

1.1 This policy governs secured and unsecured working-capital and term loans to micro, small and medium enterprises, and to their proprietors where the business income is the source of repayment.

1.2 Where this policy and a regulatory direction differ, the regulatory direction prevails.

# §3 Income assessment

## §3.1 Fixed obligation to income ratio (FOIR)

3.1.1 FOIR is the total of all monthly EMIs and fixed obligations — existing loans, the proposed loan, card minimum dues and lease rentals — divided by the assessed monthly income, expressed as a percentage.

3.1.2 Assessed income is the lower of the income evidenced by bank credits and the income evidenced by the filed ITR. Declared income on the application form is never used on its own.

3.1.3 FOIR limits by assessed monthly income:

| Assessed monthly income | Maximum FOIR | Approver above limit |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₹50,000 | 45% | Not permitted |
| ₹50,001 to ₹2,00,000 | 55% | Regional credit |
| Above ₹2,00,000 | 65% | Zonal credit committee |

3.1.4 EMIs visible on the bank statement count towards FOIR whether or not the applicant declared them.

## §3.2 Excluded credits

3.2.1 The following are excluded from assessed income:
(a) credits returned to the payer, or to a party linked to the payer, within 7 days;
(b) loan disbursals and transfers between the applicant's own accounts;
(c) one-off credits in the 30 days before application that exceed the average monthly credit, unless their source is documented.

# §4 Exposure norms

## §4.2 Gambling and speculative spend

4.2.1 Spend on betting, fantasy-gaming or crypto platforms is permitted unless:
(a) it exceeds 5% of average monthly credits; or
(b) the applicant has a prior default in the last 24 months.

4.2.2 Any case breaching 4.2.1 requires senior credit approval and a documented rationale on the case file.

# §5 Documentation

## §5.1 Bank statements

5.1.1 Statements must cover the last 12 months for all operative accounts. Statements fetched through the Account Aggregator framework are preferred; an uploaded PDF is accepted only where AA consent is refused or the bank is not on the network.

5.1.2 An uploaded statement with a balance discontinuity, page carry-over mismatch or totals mismatch is rejected, and the statement is re-obtained directly from the bank.

## §5.3 Tax filings

5.3.1 ITR acknowledgements and computations are required for the last two assessment years.

5.3.2 Borrowers liable to tax audit under section 44AB of the Income-tax Act, 1961 must also furnish the tax audit report — Form 3CA or 3CB with Form 3CD — for the same years. A 44AB-liable borrower without an audit report is not eligible for a limit above ₹50 lakh.

5.3.3 GST returns (GSTR-3B) for the last 12 months are required for GST-registered borrowers. Turnover is compared with ITR business turnover, not with gross total income.

# §6 Verification

6.1 The credit officer verifies every uploaded document against its source where a source exists: statements against Account Aggregator data, ITRs against the filed return, employer and company details against the MCA register.

6.2 A finding confirmed by the investigation team places the application on hold. A finding of fabrication or tampering is reported to the fraud monitoring cell within 7 days.

6.3 Dismissing a finding requires a written reason on the case file.

# §7 Escalation matrix

| Situation | Escalate to | Within |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed tampering or fabrication | Fraud monitoring cell | 7 days |
| FOIR above limit, income above ₹2,00,000 | Zonal credit committee | Before sanction |
| Gambling spend above 5% of credits | Senior credit approver | Before sanction |
| Same document found in another application | Investigation team | 2 working days |
